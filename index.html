Signed order form in. Billing schedule and customer setup out.

Billing and onboarding both work from the order form. This reads it once, checks it against a rubric, flags anything that blocks the first invoice, and turns it into an invoice schedule, a setup checklist and CRM fields. Nothing leaves your browser.

Runs entirely in the browser: pasted text and PDFs are never uploaded. Supported hardware and export formats are taken from mach9.ai (2 Nov 2026) and are editable in the rubric. Billing math: discounts apply to recurring fees only; one-time fees go on the first invoice, or on their own invoice at start when billing is in arrears; usage fees are billed on actuals and kept out of contract value.
